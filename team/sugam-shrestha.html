About Sugam Shrestha

Sugam Shrestha founded ABC Trek in Nepal with a clear, singular ambition: to build a specialized mountain operator free from the impersonal shortcuts that characterize modern mass tourism. In an industry where online agencies routinely pool strangers into rushed itineraries and subcontract field execution to unvetted third parties, Sugam envisioned an ethical, specialist model anchored in conservative high-altitude physiology and intimate local knowledge.

As Expedition Director, Sugam oversees the entire lifecycle of each trekking group. From the initial pre-departure consultation and gear appraisal in Kathmandu, to calculating acclimatization climb gradients through the Modi Khola river gorge, every itinerary is engineered to minimize altitude distress and maximize trail immersion. He personally selects and supervises our licensed local guides and porters, ensuring each team member is equipped with high-altitude medical gear, fair living wages, and comprehensive insurance.

As Lead Mountain Specialist, Sugam’s intuitive command of the Annapurna geography stems from more than 10 years of walking these trails in every season. He understands how microclimates behave between the humid rhododendron forests of Chomrong and the icy alpine bowl of Machapuchare Base Camp (3,700m). This first-hand perspective allows him to anticipate seasonal snow corridors, identify safe avalanche diversion paths, and hand-reserve prime teahouse accommodations before commercial crowds arrive.

“The Himalayas do not yield to impatience. A true mountain leader does not measure success merely by standing on the summit or reaching Base Camp; success is measured by bringing every traveler, guide, and porter safely home with dignity, awe, and a lasting respect for these mountains.”

Beyond safety and logistics, Sugam’s work is deeply grounded in the cultural fabric of the Annapurna sanctuary. Born in Nepal with family ties to mountain communities, he maintains longstanding friendships with the Gurung, Magar, and Thakali elders whose teahouses line the trails. By championing localized economic benefits and Leave No Trace (LNT) trail ethics, Sugam ensures that every expedition supports the mountain communities who call this sacred landscape home.

The Principles of Safe Guidance

Leadership in the Mountains

01. Safety Over Schedule

The mountain dictates our timeline. If a high pass is shrouded in an active squall or a trekker presents early symptoms of Acute Mountain Sickness (AMS), Sugam empowers guides to halt or descend immediately. There is zero stigma in pausing; turning around when conditions dictate is the hallmark of genuine mountain leadership.

02. Transparent Teamwork

Every morning begins with an honest trail briefing: expected elevation gain, water points, weather forecasts, and trail hazards. Trekkers are active partners in their journey, not passive followers. Open communication builds the trust essential for safe high-altitude travel.

03. Porter Dignity & Ethical Care

Porters are the resilient backbone of Himalayan trekking. Sugam guarantees that every porter on an ABC Trek expedition carries a capped maximum load of 20kg, receives warm alpine outerwear, has paid medical coverage, and eats nutritious meals equal to the guiding staff.

04. Ecological Stewardship

Adhering strictly to Leave No Trace (LNT) principles. We eliminate single-use plastics across our expeditions by promoting boiled filtered water, pack out all non-biodegradable waste, and support solar-powered teahouses throughout the Annapurna Sanctuary.

The Journey of a Mountain Specialist

Sugam's Story: From Foothills to Sanctuary Leader

Sugam’s relationship with the mountains did not begin in an executive boardroom; it began with the rhythm of walking. Growing up in Nepal, the high snow-capped ridges of the Himalaya were a constant presence on the horizon, instilling an early reverence for the raw scale of the landscape and the resilient people who inhabit its steep valleys.

His guiding career started at the grassroots level. Rather than jumping directly into managerial positions, Sugam spent his early years on the trail working alongside seasoned Sherpa and Gurung mountain veterans. He learned the craft from the ground up: carrying loads, understanding teahouse kitchen dynamics, reading early-morning cloud formations over Machapuchare, and mastering the subtle art of pacing trekkers who were taking their very first steps above 3,000 meters.

“In the mountains, you learn humility very quickly. Nature doesn’t care about your ego or your schedule. What matters is patience, preparation, and looking after the person walking next to you.”

Over the course of a decade and more than 120 high-altitude expeditions, Sugam witnessed first-hand how mass commercialization was beginning to erode the trekking experience. Agencies were slashing prices by cutting porter gear, skipping critical acclimatization days, and cramming travelers into rigid itineraries that resulted in unnecessary mountain sickness evacuations.

Determined to establish a higher benchmark, Sugam founded ABC Trek in Nepal as a dedicated, specialized agency focused specifically on the Annapurna Conservation Area. His vision was simple: operate smaller group sizes, provide conservative acclimatization ascent gradients, employ certified local guides with fair wages, and maintain direct, personal communication with every international traveler from the first inquiry to the final airport farewell.

Today, as Founder and Expedition Director, Sugam remains actively engaged with the mountains. Whether conducting winter safety reconnaissance through the Modi Khola canyon or welcoming returning clients at our Kathmandu headquarters in Budhanilkantha, his mission remains constant: sharing the transformative power of the Annapurna Himalaya with safety, integrity, and genuine human connection.
