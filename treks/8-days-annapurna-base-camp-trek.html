8 Days Annapurna Base Camp Trek – Overview

The 8 Days Annapurna Base Camp trek offers an efficient route into the heart of the Annapurna Sanctuary, cutting out the lower loop to focus purely on the glacial mountain amphitheater.

By starting at the roadhead of Siwai (via private 4WD from Pokhara), this itinerary climbs straight up through Chhomrong, Bamboo, and Deurali directly to Machhapuchhre Base Camp (3,700m) and Annapurna Base Camp (4,130m). It provides an ideal balance of fast pacing without sacrificing acclimatization.

Base Camp Amphitheater

Witness dawn light across the 360-degree snow walls of Annapurna I and Annapurna South.

Efficient Trailhead Access

Save 2 days of lower valley walking via private 4WD transit to Siwai.

Jhinu Hot Springs

Soothe tired legs in natural riverside hot spring pools on your descent.

Gurung Hospitality

Stay in classic Gurung hospitality teahouses in upper Chhomrong and Sinuwa.

Field Schedule

Day-by-Day Expedition Itinerary

Paced for optimal safety, terrain enjoyment, and cultural immersion.

Scenic 4WD drive to Siwai trailhead. Ascend through Jhinu Danda to the stone village of Chhomrong.

Lead Guide Field Note

Prepare for stone staircases up to Chhomrong. Hydrate well.

Descend Chhomrong stone steps, cross suspension bridge, climb to Sinuwa and continue to Dovan.

Trek through subalpine bamboo and oak forests past the historic rock shelter of Hinku Cave to Deurali.

Enter the narrow gateway into the Sanctuary. The gorge widens dramatically as you reach MBC.

Lead Guide Field Note

Afternoon SpO2 pulse oximetry monitoring conducted by your lead guide.

Gentle 2-hour walk into the glacial basin of ABC. Sunset over the south face of Annapurna I (8,091m).

Golden sunrise over Annapurna amphitheater. Descend through MBC, Deurali, and Himalaya down to Bamboo.

Climb up to Chhomrong for lunch, descend to Jhinu. Afternoon relaxation in riverside hot spring pools.

Cross the long Jhinu suspension bridge to Siwai. Private 4WD transfer back to Pokhara Lakeside.
