Medical & Evacuation Leadership

The Highest Altitude Safety Standard on Trail

High-altitude trekking requires clinical discipline, not guesswork. Here is how our team protects your health every single day.

01

Daily Pulse Oximetry Logs

Every morning and evening, your lead guide records your blood oxygen saturation (SpO2) and resting heart rate to establish your personal acclimatization baseline.

02

Lake Louise AMS Protocol

We evaluate symptoms using the international Lake Louise Acute Mountain Sickness consensus scoring. If score thresholds are exceeded, immediate descent protocols are initiated.

03

Direct Helicopter Dispatch

Direct emergency coordination with helicopter rescue operators in Pokhara and Kathmandu, ensuring rapid extraction capabilities from designated helipads in the Sanctuary.
