Expedition Architecture

A Masterclass in High-Altitude Pacing

The Annapurna Base Camp trek offers one of the world's most dramatic amphitheaters. Unlike rapid itineraries that push trekkers recklessly up the Modi Khola canyon, our 10-day route guarantees conservative ascent gradients and adequate sleep thresholds below 3,300 meters before entering the high sanctuary.

You traverse diverse ecological zones: from subtropical terraced paddy fields through dense rhododendron and bamboo forests, culminating in the glacial moraines of Machhapuchhre Base Camp (3,700m) and Annapurna Base Camp (4,130m).

Field Schedule

Day-by-Day Interactive Itinerary

Every day incorporates transparent walking telemetry, elevation differentials, terrain preview, and actionable guide notes.

After an early breakfast in Pokhara, board our private 4WD vehicle for a scenic 1.5-hour drive to the trailhead at Nayapul. Clear permit checkpoints at Birethanti (ACAP office) where our guide registers your conservation permits. Begin walking along the Modi Khola riverbed before beginning the stone staircase ascent toward Tikhedhunga.

Lead Guide Field Note

The 3,300 stone steps of Ulleri start today. Do not rush. Maintain a steady, rhythmic breathing cadence and use trekking poles to relieve knee pressure.

Ascend through primeval rhododendron and oak moss forests accompanied by the sound of mountain cascades. As we gain elevation toward Ghorepani, the temperatures drop and views of Annapurna South (7,219m) and Hiunchuli begin to reveal themselves.

Lead Guide Field Note

Ghorepani sits at 2,860m. Evenings are cold. Hydrate with at least 3 liters of boiled water; your body loses significant moisture in cold air.

Total Financial Transparency

What Is Included & What Is Excluded

No hidden fees or surprises on the trail. Clear demarcation of all operational inclusions versus personal incidental expenses.

Everything Included in Package
  • All Permits: Annapurna Conservation Area Project (ACAP) permit and government registration fees.
  • Expert Mountain Guide: Licensed, English-fluent Himalayan guide certified in Wilderness First Aid.
  • Ethical Porters (IPPG Compliant): 1 porter per 2 trekkers, maximum 20kg load cap, fair living wages, and full cold-weather gear.
  • Full Teahouse Accommodation: Twin-share rooms in vetted, clean mountain lodges across all 9 nights on trail.
  • All Trail Meals: 3 freshly prepared meals per day (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner) plus seasonal fresh fruits and hot tea.
  • Private Ground Transport: Pokhara to Nayapul/Siwai and return via comfortable private 4WD Scorpio jeep.
Exclusions & Incidental Cash Needs
  • International Flights & Visa: Nepal tourist entry visa ($30 for 15 days / $50 for 30 days payable on arrival in Kathmandu).
  • Travel & Rescue Insurance: Mandatory policy covering high-altitude trekking up to 4,500m and emergency helicopter extraction.
  • Teahouse Incidentals: Hot showers (NPR 300–500), battery charging (NPR 200–400), and Wi-Fi access cards.
  • Personal Trekking Gear: Down jacket, trekking boots, sleeping bag (all available for rental in Pokhara at ~$2/day).
  • Tips for Crew: Discretionary gratuity for guide and porters (customarily ~$10–$15 per day total).
