The Specialist Creed

Dedicated to the Annapurna Sanctuary

We founded ABC Trek in Nepal with a singular mission: to provide international adventurers with the finest, safest, and most ethically operated Annapurna Base Camp expeditions in the Himalayas.

Our Story: Why Focus Exclusively on Annapurna?

The global trekking industry is dominated by aggregators offering tours to 50 countries and 500 trails. On those portals, Annapurna Base Camp is treated as an interchangeable commodity.

We chose the opposite path: radical specialization. By dedicating our operations solely to the Annapurna region and its natural Sanctuary basin, we control every variable of the journey:

  • We retain the finest licensed Sherpa and Gurung guides who have grown up in these valleys.
  • We maintain hand-selected relationships with the cleanest, highest-altitude teahouses in Chhomrong, Deurali, and ABC.
  • We ensure every single porter receives fair wages, proper cold-weather clothing, and full medical rescue insurance.
  • We design itineraries with conservative ascent gradients that dramatically reduce altitude sickness.

Our 4 Uncompromising Standards

1. Clinical Altitude Safety

Daily SpO2 pulse oximeter monitoring and Lake Louise AMS scoring to catch early altitude complications.

2. Ethical Porter Stewardship

Strict compliance with IPPG guidelines: 20kg maximum weight cap, full gear, fair living wages, and insurance.

3. Direct Operational Transparency

Zero hidden local fees. All ACAP permits, private 4WD transit, lodging, and meals are clearly itemized.

4. Flexible Rebooking Guarantee

Free trip postponement or date rescheduling up to 30 days prior if your travel plans change.
